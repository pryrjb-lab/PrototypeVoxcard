What is VoxCard?

VoxCard is a digital business card platform. You get a single link that holds your contact info, social profiles, and booking page — shareable by text, DM, or NFC tap.

How is it different from Linktree?

While Linktree is great for social bios, VoxCard is built specifically for professional networking, offering direct contact saving (vCard), QR code generation, and physical NFC card integration.

Do people need an app to view my card?

No! Your profile opens directly in their smartphone's native web browser. No app downloads required for you or your connections.

Can I update my profile after ordering?
